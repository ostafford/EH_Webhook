How it works & why

Employee details, entered once in Connecteam, kept in step with Employment Hero Payroll

A plain-language walkthrough for the person approving onboarding and watching the alerts — no technical background needed.

  • One direction only
  • Runs on its own
  • Checks every minute
  • Bank & TFN never stored

The problem it removes

Today the same personal details are typed twice — once by the employee in Connecteam, then again by an administrator into Employment Hero Payroll. Re-typing is slow and it is where mistakes creep in. This connector does the second entry automatically, so the employee’s answers in Connecteam are the single source of truth.

The journey of one person’s details

1
In Connecteam

The employee fills in their onboarding pack

Name, date of birth, address, emergency contact, bank account, super fund, tax file number and tax questions — all entered once, by the person they belong to.

2
In Connecteam · a manager

A manager approves the onboarding

Nothing is sent to payroll until this happens. Approval is the green light — it means a person has looked over the details first.

3
The connector

The connector transfers the details

Within about a minute of approval, it takes each Connecteam answer and puts it in the matching Employment Hero field — “Legal Surname” into “Surname”, and so on.

Bank details and the tax file number pass straight through. The connector never keeps a copy of them.

4
In Employment Hero Payroll

The employee record appears — and stays current

Created the first time. After that, whenever the employee changes something in their Connecteam profile, the same change is made in Employment Hero automatically.

If Employment Hero rejects something

Say a bank BSB that doesn’t add up. Employment Hero refuses it, and the employee gets a Connecteam chat message naming exactly what to fix. They correct it in Connecteam, and the connector tries again on its own. The details in Connecteam and Employment Hero never drift apart.

Why it’s built this way

Enter once, in Connecteam

The employee already uses Connecteam for onboarding. Asking them to fill it in twice is the exact waste we’re removing.

Start only at approval

A half-finished pack shouldn’t reach payroll. Tying the sync to the approval you already do means a human has always checked first.

Mistakes go back to the person who made them

The employee sees the problem in Connecteam chat, where they already work, and can fix it themselves in seconds — no admin ticket, no email thread.

It checks by itself, every minute

No one has to remember to press a button or run a report. Approve the pack and walk away.

Sensitive details are never stored

Bank accounts and tax file numbers travel through the connector and are gone the moment they’re delivered. Nothing sensitive sits in the middle.

One direction, on purpose

Connecteam is the source of truth. Employment Hero is never written back to Connecteam, so there’s no confusion about which system is right.

What you’ll see

Three kinds of message can come out of the connector. Knowing which is which tells you who needs to act.

To the employee “Please fix this” They mistyped something and Employment Hero wouldn’t accept it. They correct it in Connecteam; the connector retries automatically. No action from you.
To your alerts channel “Needs a person” The details are fine, but something has to be set in Employment Hero by hand — for example a non-resident tax scale, or a self-managed super fund. Payroll finishes it off in Employment Hero.
To your alerts channel “Something isn’t working” A technical fault — the connector couldn’t reach Employment Hero, or a key expired. This is one to pass to whoever maintains the integration.
Not included, by choice. Offboarding and terminations, pay rates, and award setup stay manual in Employment Hero for now. They involve payroll judgement that shouldn’t be automated in the first version.
